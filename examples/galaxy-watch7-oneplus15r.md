# Initial device pair

- Watch: Galaxy Watch7 44 mm LTE, 480×480; later inspected Android 36 / Wear OS 6.
- Phone: OnePlus 15R / CPH2767.
- Controller source baseline: watch 0.22-manual, phone 0.15-manual.
- Bluetooth selection and manual helper connection: observed.
- Dedicated controller pinch NEXT and gentle turn-and-return PREV: user confirmed before native gestures were turned off.
- Home-screen control: rejected after navigation/complication interference; removed.
- Manual-session shutdown: connected link → close watch activity → both services absent, phone helper disabled.
- Final preference: Samsung Universal Gestures off; both controllers stopped; development debugging off.
- Battery endurance and numerical recognition accuracy: unmeasured.

This record summarizes testing without publishing private logs or identifiers. It does not assert that rebuilt public APKs with a new development key were installed on the original devices.
